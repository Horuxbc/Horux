Part 1 · Case study — silent reading

The Missing Work Order

You are the Administrative Assistant at Altru Roofing, a roofing company in Oklahoma. Your role is to coordinate and support the scheduling and execution of roofing projects once the Sales Representative confirms a start date with the homeowner.

The owner of the company, Noah, is highly involved in day-to-day operations. He is action-oriented, often jumps in to solve problems himself, and occasionally forgets to communicate or delegate tasks because it feels faster to handle them personally.

One Tuesday morning at 6:00 AM, Noah realizes that a roofing project scheduled for that day is missing materials. Upon investigating, he finds that the Work Order was never sent to the supplier. As a result:

  • Materials will not arrive on time.
  • Noah decides to pick up the materials himself and personally delivers them to the crew.
  • To help the team recover lost time, he also purchases water and lunch for the roofers.
  • The project starts later than expected, creating stress for both the crew and the homeowner.

You will discuss these three questions:

Q1First hour. If you discovered this at 6:00 AM, what would you do during the first hour to minimize the impact on the project, crew, homeowner, and owner — and why in that order?
Q2Prevention. Design a simple process that would keep this from happening again. Be ready to show how you would actually set it up.
Q3Working with Noah. Knowing Noah often handles tasks himself and sometimes forgets to delegate, what would you do to keep him informed while taking real ownership of operations?

Read carefully. The notes phase will begin automatically when the timer ends.
